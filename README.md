In this repository, I store my university project from the Business Mathematics specialisation. This project was part of the last course "Statistics" and that every student did on their own. The main objective of this project was to perform linear regression on some real life dataset. For my work, I decided to study the real estate market of Melbourne, state of Victoria, Australia.

---
In this repository, you can find a Rmd and a PDF file. For quick check on the project, I suggest you look at the PDF, but in case you want to examine it more deeply, or at least if you aren't satisfied with quality of the plots, you can download the Rmd file and compile it in your preferred app.
